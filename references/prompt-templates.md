# Prompt Templates

## Template: Daily post-close recap

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill.
Create a 60-second vertical stock-market recap for the Indian market, with Ren as a bottom-right waist-up anchor.
Use the official NSE/BSE closes for the day: Nifty 50, Sensex, the leading and lagging sectors, the biggest gainers and losers, FII/DII flows, and the rupee, crude and VIX moves that mattered.
Build a price ledger with a source and an as-of timestamp for every number, then write the 10-beat lineup, the narration, and the scene plan with price cards and charts.
No investment advice — facts, moves and the calendar only. Do not assume a Remotion plugin exists.
```

## Template: Pre-market brief

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill in pre-market mode.
Create a 60-second vertical brief for today's Indian session: GIFT Nifty, the overnight US close, major Asian markets, the day's earnings and macro calendar, and the levels traders are watching.
Timestamp everything, keep it factual, and end with the disclaimer. Ren bottom-right, origami style throughout.
```

## Template: Movers only

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill.
Build a 60-second vertical short on today's top five gainers and top five losers from the Nifty 500, one beat each, with percent moves, the catalyst for each, and a price card per beat.
Skip the index commentary. Numbers from the price ledger, no advice.
```

## Template: Explain one theme

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill.
Explain in 60 seconds why the banking sector moved the index today: the sector index move, the two or three stocks that drove it, the catalyst, and what it means for the wider market.
Origami visuals, Ren bottom-right, factual tone, no recommendations.
```

## Template: Only create the editorial plan

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill, but stop before rendering.
Create the 10-beat lineup, the price ledger with sources and timestamps, the 60-second timing plan, and a scene-by-scene storyboard for an origami market recap with Ren.
```

## Template: Only create Ren overlays

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill.
Create Ren overlays for the listed market beats using the bundled Ren character engine. Match his emotion to the market's tone, keep him clear of the price cards, and export the needed stills or scene states for the editor.
```

## Template: Update an existing episode

```text
Use the Origami Market Video skill.
Refresh the existing episode with the new session's closes: replace the changed numbers and beats, re-timestamp every as-of label, keep the origami design system, Ren continuity and scene timing, and rerun the validator before export.
```
